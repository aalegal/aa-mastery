Looking after yourself is part of the work

Document review asks you to concentrate hard, for long stretches, sometimes on material that is genuinely unpleasant. That takes something out of you. Noticing it early is a skill, not a weakness.

Nothing on this page is tracked, scored, or reported. Read what's useful and ignore the rest.

Responding well when someone isn't okay

You don't need to be a counsellor. Most of what helps is ordinary: noticing, asking plainly, listening without rushing to fix, and adjusting what you can actually control — the work.

This track covers what to look for, what to say, what not to say, and where your role stops.

If you need real help

This site is a training resource. It is not a counselling service and it cannot help in an emergency. The people below can.

🚨
If someone's life is in danger right now — go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or get someone to take you. In Tanzania, ambulance response can be slow or unavailable outside the major cities, so travelling directly to a hospital is usually faster than waiting.
Talk to someone today
Afya Call Center
199
Tanzania's Ministry of Health national health line. Free, 24 hours, confidential. Works from any phone, including a basic feature phone, by call or SMS. Mental health is one of the areas they advise on, alongside general health. This is the best first call if you're not sure where to start.
Mirembe National Mental Health Hospital — Dodoma
Tanzania's national referral hospital for mental health, and the country's only specialist mental health hospital. Provides general adult psychiatry, substance use services, and neuropsychiatric care. Appropriate for ongoing treatment or a referral, rather than an immediate crisis.
Emergency numbers
112 — police and general emergencies. 114 — ambulance.
Be aware that coverage is patchy outside Dar es Salaam and response times can be slow. Getting yourself to a hospital directly is often the faster route.
116 — National Child Helpline
Free and 24 hours, but aimed at children and young people. Listed here in case it's useful for someone in your family rather than for you.
